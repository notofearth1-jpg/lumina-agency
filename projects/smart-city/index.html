Smart City OS

The operating system for modern urban life. Connecting citizens, infrastructure, and services into a unified, responsive ecosystem.

Client

City of Tomorrow

Services

  • Mobile Strategy
  • IoT Integration
  • Geospatial Data

The Challenge

Urban services are often fragmented—finding transit, reporting issues, or accessing utilities requires multiple disconnected apps. The goal was to unify these functions into a single, seamless platform.

The Solution

Smart City OS integrates real-time transit data, IoT sensor feeds (like air quality and parking), and municipal services. A modular dashboard adapts to the user's location and needs in real-time.
