AR/VR Learning Platform

Transforming education through immersive technology. Bringing history to life and abstract concepts into focus with augmented and virtual reality.

Client

EduVerse

Services

  • AR/VR Design
  • 3D Modeling
  • Mobile Dev

Industry

Education Tech

The Challenge

Traditional learning materials can struggle to convey complex spatial or historical concepts. The goal was to create an engaging, interactive platform that bridges the gap between textbooks and reality.

The Solution

We developed a dual-mode application. The AR mode overlays digital information onto the real world using the phone's camera, while the VR mode transports students to entirely new environments for immersive field trips.
