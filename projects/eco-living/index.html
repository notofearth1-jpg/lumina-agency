Focus

Sustainability

The Challenge

Climate change feels overwhelming, and individual contributions can seem insignificant. The challenge was to gamify sustainability and provide clear, actionable insights into daily habits.

The Solution

We created a serene, nature-inspired interface that uses positive reinforcement. The app tracks carbon footprint across transport, diet, and energy, offering personalized tips and visualizing collective impact.
