Healthcare Companion

Bridging the gap between patient and provider. Real-time vitals monitoring, secure telemedicine, and personalized care plans.

Client

MediConnect

Services

  • HIPAA Compliant Dev
  • UI/UX Design
  • Data Security

The Challenge

Healthcare is fragmented. Patients lose track of medications, appointments, and test results. We needed a centralized, secure platform that empowers patients to take control of their health journey.

The Solution

A unified health dashboard that integrates with wearable devices for real-time monitoring. Features include secure doctor-patient messaging, automatic medication reminders, and AI-driven symptom analysis.
